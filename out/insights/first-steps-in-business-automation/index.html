Three First Steps in Business Automation

Business Automation · 20 October 2026 · 3 min read

Begin by writing down tasks that are repetitive, rule-based and frequent. Those are the best candidates.

Pick one, map each step, and decide what should trigger it. Automation is easier when the trigger and the result are both clear.

Run the automated version beside the manual one for a short period, compare, then switch over. Review what it saves and choose the next one.
